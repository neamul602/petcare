- **Polling:** Fit numbers re-validate every 60s + on window focus (Fit updates after each completion)

## On-Page State Machine

- **State 1 (default — strong history):** All stats populated · no "sparse" placeholder · delta indicator shows recent climbs
- **State 2 (sparse history, new Tier-A):** Empty-state placeholder "No demonstrated-Fit yet · your profile shows self-declared capability until your first bookings complete" · stats hidden · timeline shows zero entries
- **State 3 (filtered view):** Filter chip selected · timeline re-renders to matching entries · stat cards may show subset totals
- **State 4 (methodology open):** Modal open · Fit calculation explained · "Close" or tap-outside closes

## Accessibility

- Stats: `<dl>` semantic · each stat is `<dt>`/`<dd>` · `aria-label` provides full context (e.g., "Diabetic small dogs handled: 13")
- Delta indicator: `role="status" aria-live="polite"` when Fit climbs this session
- Filter chips: `role="button" aria-pressed` reflects state · keyboard-navigable
- Care-record entries: `<article>` semantic · tap target wraps entire card
- Methodology modal: native `<dialog>` · focus trap · Escape closes
- Methodology content: structured with `<h2>`/`h3` headers · lists use semantic markup
- All numbers use `tabular-nums` for visual stability
- All CTAs ≥48px tap target

## What This Page Must NOT Do

- No gamification of Fit numbers (Fit is informational, not a leaderboard)
- No fake Fit claims (data must come from completed bookings + verified reviews)
- No provider comparison ("Tier-A providers in Gulshan average 8 diabetic pets handled")
- No pressure CTAs ("Handle 2 more diabetic pets to reach gold tier")
- No methodology gatekeeping (link is always visible · informational only)
