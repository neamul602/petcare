# 3.5 · Spec — In-Stay Management

> The full in-stay picture on one screen — daily updates, booking chat, vet directory, acute-event trigger. Rashid manages the stay from his phone without leaving the app. **The structural answer to Bibi Fear #3 (radio silence after handover).**

## URL & Routing

- **Route:** `/provider/bookings/:bookingId/in-stay` · mobile-only · deep-linkable from dashboard in-stay cards
- **Polling:** every 60s for chat messages + daily-update status (lightweight, low data)
- **Push:** subscribe to `booking.{id}.messages` and `booking.{id}.events` channels

## Design Sections

**D1. Header** — back to dashboard · "Tito's stay · Day N of M" · `tabular text-sm`

**D2. Pet card** — `{card.muted}` · thumbnail + identity + insulin-due countdown · medical flag chip (Diabetes · `{badge.warning-soft}`)

**D3. Today's daily-update prompt** — `{card.elevated}` · status badge + Post CTA
- "Due today" · `{badge.warning-soft}` · soft reminder, not blocking
- "Posted" · `{success-soft}` · green check · CTA changes to "Edit today's update"
- "Skipped (grace allowed)" · `{badge.neutral}` · no penalty

**D4. Past updates (chronological)** — list of `{card.muted}` per day · photo grid + structured note
- Each card: date + timestamp · photo grid (1:1 thumbs) · note quote · care-flag chips (Glucose, Insulin, Behaviour)
- Most recent at top · scrollable

**D5. Chat snippet** — `{card.muted}` · last 2 messages + "Open full chat →" link
- Chat bubble styling uses DESIGN.md `chat-bubble` tokens
- Booking-scoped chat (out of scope for full thread, snippet here)

**D6. Vet directory** — pinned section · `{vet-clinic-entry}` component (DESIGN.md) · always expanded · phone-pickable · 2 alternative clinics listed

**D7. Acute-event trigger** — `{destructive-soft}` banner · CTA "If something is wrong, tap here" · routes to provider-side acute-event runbook trigger surface — see [`../../04-ayaats-eid-week-runbook-execution/4.2-acute-event-runbook/4.2-acute-event-runbook.spec.md`](../../04-ayaats-eid-week-runbook-execution/4.2-acute-event-runbook/4.2-acute-event-runbook.spec.md) (R-10 launch-blocking · Scenario 04)

## On-Page State Machine

- **State 1 (default — daily-update posted today):** Status shows green check · CTA = "Edit today's update" · past updates populated
- **State 2 (daily-update due today, not yet posted):** Status shows "Due today" · CTA = "Post today's update" · soft reminder banner visible
- **State 3 (daily-update overdue — past midnight, not posted):** Status shows "Skipped (grace allowed)" · no penalty · CTA still available · doesn't block completion
- **State 4 (acute-event active):** Banner replaces update section with runbook entry · `{destructive-soft}` styling
- **State 5 (connectivity-issue, load-shedding):** Banner "Connection intermittent · updates will sync when restored" · past updates still visible
- **State 6 (chat-new-message):** Snippet updates with unread badge · "Open full chat" highlighted

## Accessibility

- Pet card: `<header>` semantic · insulin-due `role="timer" aria-live="off"`
- Daily-update prompt: `<section role="status">` · announces when status changes
- Past-update cards: `<article>` semantic · photo grid `<ul>` with `<li>` + descriptive alt
- Chat snippet: `<section aria-label="Recent chat with Bibi">`
- Vet directory: phone link `<a href="tel:...">` · `aria-label="Call Dr. {Name}"`
- Acute-event trigger: `role="alert"` banner · routes to Scenario 04 surface
- Connectivity banner: `role="status" aria-live="polite"`
- All CTAs ≥48px tap target

## What This Page Must NOT Do

- No blocking stay completion on missed updates (Brief §3: soft expectation, not system-enforced)
- No auto-generated updates (Rashid's voice, his words, his photos — authenticity is the signal)
- No scheduled updates (soft-enforcement only works if updates are real-time)
- No penalty for missed updates (this is Rashid's surface, not Bibi's)
- No gamification of update frequency (stays informational)
