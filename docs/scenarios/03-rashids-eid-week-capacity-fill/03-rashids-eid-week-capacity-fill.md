# Scenario 03 — Rashid's Eid-Week Capacity Fill + Care-Record Credit

> The supply foundation. If Bibi's repeat booking is the engine, Rashid's accumulated care-record is the trust signal Bibi's first search surfaces.

---

## The 8 Strategic Questions

### Q1. What transaction do we need to get really right?
Receive, confirm, and complete multiple special-care bookings during Eid-week compression — while accumulating demonstrated-Fit evidence into Rashid's profile so the right owners find him next time.

### Q2. If this transaction succeeds, which business goal does it add value to?
🚀 **Secondary Objective — Booking volume clears operational costs without breaching the 1/25/75 ceiling** AND 🌱 **Tertiary Objective — Demonstrated Care Fit replaces self-declared Capability as the trust signal**. Rashid's completed special-care stays compound into demonstrated-Fit that Bibi's first search surfaces — closing the loop on Primary Objective 1.

**Anchored FRs / SMs:** FR-4 (provider application + approval), FR-5 (Tier-A profile), FR-12 (date-level capacity), FR-14 (acute-event backup), FR-15 (daily updates), FR-19 (verified reviews with photos), FR-21 (review-with-photos), FR-22 (acute-event escalation), FR-26 (chat + audit), FR-49 (demonstrated-Fit), FR-50 (off-platform soft-signal), SM-7 (review-with-photos rate), SM-11 (demonstrated-Fit exposure), SM-13 (off-peak utilization).

### Q3. Which user experiences this most, and in what real-life situation?

**Persona:** 🔧 Rashid the Reliable-Reliant (Secondary · Provider)
**Profile:** Gulshan home-sitter, 6 years hosting, Tier-A on PetCare since launch. Multi-species handled; dedicated pet room + catio; 4.9★ with photos. Wary of being reduced to a commodity by an algorithm he doesn't control.
**Situation:** Eid-week compression — he's adjusted his date-level capacity for the peak window (added 2 spots per night for the Eid-1 → Eid+9 window). He expects a queue of incoming requests; wants to confirm good-fit bookings fast; wants his daily updates to be auditable and to count toward his profile.

### Q4. What do they want and what do they fear?

- **Hope:** The incoming-request queue shows pet profile + care config upfront so he can confirm/decline fast without WhatsApp back-and-forth; date-level capacity is real (no overbooking); daily updates accumulate into demonstrated-Fit on his profile; payout cadence is predictable.
- **Worry:** Off-peak weeks starve him of bookings (he keeps his dedicated pet room for nothing); owners book once and take his number to WhatsApp (his Fit stops accruing); an acute event happens without a clear runbook and he absorbs the blame; the algorithm ranks by self-declared capability not by what he has actually handled.

### Q5. What device are they on?
**Mobile + desktop** — primary action (confirming requests, posting daily updates) is on mobile PWA while pet is in his home; capacity calendar + payouts are on desktop web. Both are Bangla-first.

### Q6. What's the natural starting point?
Rashid opens the PetCare PWA from his phone at home. Lands on Provider dashboard. Sees the Eid-week queue with 4 pending requests, 6 confirmed bookings this week, 1 in-stay currently.

### Q7. What does the best possible outcome look like — for both sides?

- **User Success:** Rashid confirms 4 well-fit bookings in one sitting with full pet profile + care context visible. Posts daily photo-updates of Tito in the dedicated pet room with the insulin log + low-carb kibble portion visible — auditable, accumulating into his profile. Completes the stay, marks complete, payout released net of commission. Eid-week utilization at 95% of his expanded capacity.
- **Business Success:** Booking volume clears operational cost for the Eid peak. Rashid's demonstrated-Fit deepens ("diabetic small dogs handled successfully" climbs from 12 to 16). SM-13 (off-peak utilization) reads healthy. Rashid's profile becomes the canonical Dhaka reference for diabetic small-dog care.

### Q8. The shortest path (linear sunshine path — zero branches)

1. **Provider dashboard (Bookings + Capacity)** — opens Eid-week view · 4 pending requests · 6 confirmed · 1 in-stay · capacity at 95%
2. **Date-level capacity calendar** — Eid-week shows expanded capacity (5 spots/night vs typical 3) · peak-window tag visible
3. **Incoming booking request view** — Bibi's request for Tito · full pet profile + care config visible · insulin log requirement + anxiety triggers surfaced
4. **Booking confirmation decision** — Accept (one tap · structured-decline alternative available)
5. **In-stay management view** — Tito is checked in · today's daily update is pending · chat with Bibi visible · vet-clinic directory pinned
6. **Daily Update composer** — photo upload (Tito in pet room) + structured note (insulin log + kibble portion + behaviour)
7. **Booking completion (provider marks complete)** — Tito's stay completed · payout released net of commission · Bibi prompted to leave review
8. **Provider profile editor** — capability flags updated (post-stay self-reflection) · current state visible
9. **Care-record / Demonstrated-Fit history view** — "diabetic small dogs handled successfully" climbs from 12 → 13 · care-records accumulating
10. **Payouts + earnings view** — Eid-week payout summary visible · no holds · predictable cadence

✓ **Climax beat reached.**
