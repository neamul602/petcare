# 5.9 · Spec — Notifications Center (Cross-Role)

> Cross-role notifications hub — single source of truth for booking events, daily updates, re-engagement nudges, acute-event alerts, dispute status, queue alerts. Role-aware sections; each role sees what matters to them.

## URL & Routing

- **Route:** `/{role}/notifications` (e.g., `/owner/notifications`, `/provider/notifications`, `/admin/notifications`) — this spec is the role-aware template; 5.5 covers owner specifically
- **Auth gate:** role-appropriate
- **Polling:** every 30s for new notifications · push subscription on `notifications.{role}.{userId}` channel
- **Sections:** role-specific

## On-Page State Machine

- **State 1 (default — current):** Role-appropriate sections visible · unread badges populated
- **State 2 (action-required-only):** Filter applied · only action-required visible
- **State 3 (high-volume-period):** Sections group by day · "{N} updates today" summary
- **State 4 (empty-inbox):** "No notifications · We'll let you know when something happens"
- **State 5 (mark-all-read):** All unread dots cleared · confirmation toast
- **State 6 (deep-link-from-push):** Specific notification pre-expanded · scrolls to it

## Accessibility

- Header: `<header>` semantic · "Mark all as read" `aria-describedby`
- Section headers: `<h2>` semantic · `aria-live="polite"` announces new sections
- Notification cards: `<article>` semantic · CTA primary focusable
- Unread dot: `aria-label="Unread"` · not just color
- Per-notification CTAs: `aria-label="{action} for {title}"`
- Empty state: `role="status"` announces empty inbox
- All CTAs ≥48px tap target (mobile) / 40px (desktop)

## What This Page Must NOT Do

- No urgency-bait CTAs
- No pressure CTAs
- No comparison to other platforms
- No spam-frequency — role-appropriate caps per notification type
- No auto-marking-as-read on view
- No revealing other users' activity
- No showing notifications older than 6 months (archive)
