# 5.5 · Spec — Notifications Inbox (Owner)

> All owner notifications in one place — **the defection-prevention surface.** The re-engagement section is the moment of friction when WhatsApp becomes easier; this inbox keeps PetCare the path of least resistance.

## URL & Routing

- **Route:** `/owner/notifications` · deep-linkable from tab bar and from push notifications
- **Auth gate:** `owner` role
- **Polling:** every 30s for new notifications · push subscription on `notifications.owner.{userId}` channel
- **Sections:** 🔴 Action required · 🟡 Updates · 🟢 Re-engagement · ⚪ Past

## Design Sections

**D1. Header** — title · "Mark all as read" CTA · menu trigger

**D2. Section: 🔴 Action required** — `{card.elevated}` · high-priority notifications · unread badge per section

**D3. Section: 🟡 Updates** — `{card.muted}` · informational · recent updates

**D4. Section: 🟢 Re-engagement** — `{card.elevated}` with `{success-soft}` accent · **the defection-prevention surface** · "Book in under 4 min" CTA

**D5. Section: ⚪ Past** — `{card.muted}` · collapsed by default · older notifications

## On-Page State Machine

- **State 1 (default — current):** All sections visible · action required at top · re-engagement highlighted
- **State 2 (action-required-only):** Filter chip selected · only action-required visible
- **State 3 (acute-event-active):** Acute-event notification pinned at top · red banner · routes to 4.2
- **State 4 (re-engagement-active):** Re-engagement section visible with prominent CTA · "Book in under 4 min" — climax beat
- **State 5 (mark-all-read):** All unread dots cleared · confirmation toast
- **State 6 (empty-inbox):** "No notifications" illustration · "We'll let you know when something happens"
- **State 7 (high-volume):** Sections group by day · "5 updates today" summary
- **State 8 (past-collapsed):** Past section collapsed by default · "Show 12 past notifications" expand

## Accessibility

- Header: `<header>` semantic · "Mark all as read" `aria-describedby` explains action
- Section headers: `<h2>` semantic · `aria-live="polite"` announces new sections
- Notification cards: `<article>` semantic · CTA primary focusable
- Unread dot: `aria-label="Unread"` · not just color
- Re-engagement CTA: `aria-label="Book in under 4 minutes with {provider}"` · full context
- Acute-event CTA: `role="button"` · opens in-stay with event highlighted
- Past section: `<details>` semantic · collapsed by default · keyboard-expandable
- All CTAs ≥48px tap target

## What This Page Must NOT Do

- No urgency-bait CTAs ("Last chance to book!")
- No pressure CTAs ("Don't lose your pet's care history!")
- No comparison to other platforms ("WhatsApp is missing this")
- No spam-frequency — max 1 re-engagement nudge per booking per 60 days
- No auto-marking-as-read on view (manual mark-all-read only)
- No revealing other owners' activity
- No showing notifications for past bookings older than 6 months (archive)
