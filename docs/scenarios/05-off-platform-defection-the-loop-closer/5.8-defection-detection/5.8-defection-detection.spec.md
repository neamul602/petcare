# 5.8 · Spec — Defection Detection Dashboard (Admin)

> Soft signals of off-platform defection, surfaced before the moat starts dissolving. **The signal surface for the shared cross-group driver.** Counter-metrics anchored (SM-C1, SM-C2, SM-C5). Companion to 4.10 with role-aware intervention playbook.

## URL & Routing

- **Route:** `/admin/defection` · desktop-primary · deep-linkable from queue dashboard's trend-watch section
- **Auth gate:** `admin` role
- **Polling:** every 6hr for soft-signal refresh · push subscription on `defection.signals` channel

## On-Page State Machine

- **State 1 (clean-state — current):** No trending banner · counters all green · signals shown
- **State 2 (attrition-period):** Trending banner visible · playbook CTA primary
- **State 3 (counter-degraded):** At least one SM-C* counter at ⚠ or 🔴 · counter card status shifts · cross-link to playbook
- **State 4 (pattern-detected):** Cross-side signal 🔴 highlighted · high-priority intervention
- **State 5 (filter-applied):** Only matching categories visible
- **State 6 (intervention-launched):** Playbook opens (out of scope) · signal marked "intervention in progress"
- **State 7 (intervention-completed):** Signal archived · cross-link to 4.7 audit-log

## Accessibility

- Header: `<header>` semantic · summary `<dl>` semantic
- Counter metrics: `<section>` semantic · each `<dl>` semantic with `<dt>`/`<dd>`
- Trending banner: `role="status" aria-live="polite"` announces trend change
- Filter chips: `role="button" aria-pressed"` · keyboard-navigable
- Signal category cards: `<section>` semantic · each `<ul>` of signals
- Signal rows: `<li>` semantic · playbook CTA focusable
- Counter status: `aria-label="Healthy" / "Watch" / "Critical"` · not just color
- All CTAs ≥48px tap target

## What This Page Must NOT Do

- No revealing which owner/provider specifically (aggregate signals only)
- No "spy on chat" framing — soft signals, not surveillance
- No pressure CTAs ("You're losing customers!")
- No comparison to other platforms
- No auto-intervention (every nudge is admin-initiated)
- No revealing intervention to the owner/provider being nudged
- No sharing signals with other owners/providers
