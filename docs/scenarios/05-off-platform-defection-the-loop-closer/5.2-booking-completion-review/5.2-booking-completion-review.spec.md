# 5.2 · Spec — Booking Completion Confirmation (Owner Reviews)

> The demonstrated-Fit accumulator. Verified reviews with photos from the actual stay are what surface to the next owner on first search. The structural lever that makes Rashid's Fit deepen organically — and the moment Ayaat's moderation discipline (4.7) catches issues.

## URL & Routing

- **Route:** `/owner/stays/:bookingId/complete` · deep-linkable from 5.1 "Mark complete" CTA and from post-stay push notification
- **Auth gate:** `owner` role · must own the referenced booking
- **Gate:** only available after provider confirms completion OR after dispute window opens post-stay-end

## On-Page State Machine

- **State 1 (just-completed — current):** Step 1 shown · Step 2 hidden · yes/no radios
- **State 2 (yes-selected):** Step 2 form revealed · fields validated · Submit enabled when valid
- **State 3 (no-selected):** Routes to 5.3 Issue Report (independent flow per Brief §3)
- **State 4 (submit-pending):** Submit disabled · spinner · "Submitting your review..."
- **State 5 (submit-success):** Confirmation · "Review submitted · thank you" · route to dashboard · toast
- **State 6 (skip-confirmed):** Routes to dashboard · review marked as "skipped, allowed to add later"
- **State 7 (review-moderated):** If admin moderates (4.7) · cross-link to 5.4 with moderation outcome

## Accessibility

- Header: `<header>` semantic · summary `<dl>` semantic
- Step 1: `<fieldset>` with `<legend>` · radio group · arrow-key navigable
- Step 2: rating `<fieldset>` · 5 radio inputs (1-5) · `aria-label="Rating: {N} of 5 stars"`
- Textarea: `<label for>` · `aria-describedby` connects to char-count hint
- Photo upload: `<input type="file" accept="image/*" capture>` · `aria-describedby` explains requirement
- Care specifics: `<fieldset>` with `<legend>` · per-item `<label>`
- Submit CTA: `aria-disabled="true"` until valid · `aria-describedby` explains
- Skip CTA: `aria-label="Skip your review"` · confirm modal `role="dialog"`
- All CTAs ≥48px tap target

## What This Page Must NOT Do

- No review without 1+ photo (gate enforced — verified review principle)
- No review that removes/affects any existing review (Brief §3 independence)
- No pressure CTAs ("Leave a review now to help other owners!")
- No star-rating optimization (5.1/4.7/2.1 do not show "average star" as a goal)
- No public comment on the review at this point (other owners see it only after moderation passes)
- No comparison to other providers
