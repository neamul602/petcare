# Scenario 02 — Bibi's Second Booking — The Moat Proof

> The structural proof. If scenario 01 plants the seed, scenario 02 is what makes the moat compound.

---

## The 8 Strategic Questions

### Q1. What transaction do we need to get really right?
Re-book Tito's stay with Rashid — or another proven provider — by tapping "Book again" and letting PetCare's accumulated care history pre-fill the entire intake in under 4 minutes.

### Q2. If this transaction succeeds, which business goal does it add value to?
⭐ **Primary Objective — Curated-trust moat compounds through repeat bookings on-platform.** This is the compound event — second booking through PetCare (not WhatsApp), second verified review, deeper demonstrated-Fit for the chosen provider. **SM-15 (median Second-Booking Time under four minutes) is the climax metric that proves the structural value of staying on-platform.**

**Anchored FRs / SMs:** FR-7 (care history accumulation), FR-8 (pre-fill future intakes), FR-10 (platform-owned portability), FR-48 (on-platform repeat value), SM-15 (Second-Booking Time under 4 min), SM-2 (Repeat-Booking Rate).

### Q3. Which user experiences this most, and in what real-life situation?

**Persona:** 🎓 Bibi the Banani-Booker (Primary · Owner) — 4 weeks after Scenario 01's Eid booking.
**Profile:** Tito's first PetCare stay with Rashid just completed. Verified review submitted with photos. Tito came back happy, insulin curve stable, no acute events.
**Situation:** Impromptu weekend getaway — her sister's wedding in Cox's Bazar, 3 nights, Friday–Monday. Husband and kids are going; Tito can't come (heat stress + 6-hour car ride would destabilize his glucose curve). She needs boarding for Friday afternoon → Monday morning.

### Q4. What do they want and what do they fear?

- **Hope:** Tap "Book again" — intake pre-fills from Tito's care history (insulin, kibble, anxiety triggers, vet contact) — submit in under 4 minutes. Get back to packing.
- **Worry:** She has to re-explain Tito's insulin curve from scratch (the WhatsApp pain); or PetCare shows her a long form anyway because the care history didn't actually persist; or Rashid is booked for those dates and she has to choose a new provider with no demonstrated-Fit evidence yet.

### Q5. What device are they on?
**Mobile** — PWA on Android, mid-range handset. Bibi is in the kitchen, one hand on coffee, scrolling dates on her phone.

### Q6. What's the natural starting point?
Bibi opens the PetCare app from her phone's home screen (PWA bookmark). Lands on Owner dashboard. Sees "Recent booking — Tito with Rashid · Eid week · 5★" card with a prominent **"Book again"** CTA.

### Q7. What does the best possible outcome look like — for both sides?

- **User Success:** Bibi taps "Book again" → Tito's full care history pre-fills (insulin BID, low-carb kibble, anxiety triggers, vet contact, medication, behavioural notes) → she adjusts dates and adds one new dietary note → submits → total elapsed time **under 4 minutes** (SM-15 climax).
- **Business Success:** **Second booking through PetCare instead of WhatsApp.** Second verified review deepens Rashid's demonstrated-Fit. SM-2 (Repeat-Booking Rate) starts to climb. The moat is no longer aspirational — it is structurally visible to the user and the business.

### Q8. The shortest path (linear sunshine path — zero branches)

1. **Pet care history view** — Tito's profile opens to a "Recent bookings" section showing the Eid stay with Rashid (5★, photos). Tap **"Book again with Rashid"** OR tap **"Book with another provider"** OR **"Book Tito at a new stay"**.
2. **"Book again" path** — Tito's full care history pre-fills the booking configuration; Bibi adjusts dates (Friday → Monday, 3 nights), confirms the same provider (Rashid), adds one new dietary note (Tito tried salmon this week, mild reaction — flag for review); total elapsed time **under 4 minutes** ✓

✓ **Climax beat reached.**
