# 2.1 — Pet Care History View

## Page Metadata

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| **Scenario** | 02 — Bibi's Second Booking — The Moat Proof |
| **Page Number** | 2.1 |
| **Platform** | Mobile (PWA, mid-range Android) |

## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Show Bibi Tito's accumulated care history across bookings — and surface the **"Book again"** CTA as the primary action so the second-booking speed climax can begin.

**Entry Context:** Bibi tapped Tito's profile card on the Owner dashboard (or the recent-booking card post-Eid). The page is a pet-centric care history view, not a generic settings page.

**Exit Action:** Tap **"Book again with Rashid"** (or "Book again with another provider" / "Book Tito at a new stay") → next page (2.2 — Book Again Path).

**On-Page Interactions:**
- Tap "Book again with Rashid" — primary CTA, sticky
- Tap "Book again with another provider" — secondary
- Tap any past booking card — opens booking detail (out of scope for this scenario)
- Tap "Edit Tito's profile" — opens pet profile editor (1.5 in edit mode)
- Scroll to view care history (chronological)

---

## Inherited Mental State
- **Hope:** She sees Tito's full history at a glance — Eid stay with Rashid, the insulin log from the stay, the 5★ review she left, photos
- **Worry:** The history is empty or sparse — PetCare didn't actually accumulate the care data; or Rashid's profile no longer shows what he handled

## State Variations

| State | Trigger | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| **Default (post-Eid)** | Page load | Eid stay visible · "Book again with Rashid" prominent |
| **Pre-Eid (first-time user)** | Page load (no past bookings) | "No past bookings yet — Tito's first stay" + "Find care" CTA |
| **Multi-pet household** | User has multiple pets | Pet selector at top · care history per pet |
| **Provider-no-longer-available** | Rashid paused or suspended | "Rashid is currently not accepting bookings" · "Book with another provider" promoted to primary |
| **Care-history-sparse (provider declined)** | First booking declined | Care history shows decline reason (FR-24) · "Book with a different provider" CTA |

---

## What This Page Must NOT Do

- **No "Export your data" CTA beyond GDPR-style request** — the brief is explicit: the history is platform-owned, not provider-owned, and exists for the moat's structural value (Brief §6 moat-conditionality)
- **No "delete pet profile" CTA at the top level** — soft-deletion available via Settings, but not here (it would undermine the moat)
- **No upsells** — no "Upgrade to PetCare+ for multi-pet tracking" (no subscription in v1)
- **No urgency-bait** — no "Rashid's calendar fills up fast!" (brand voice discipline)
