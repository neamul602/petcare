# 1.4 — Provider Profile — Public

## Page Metadata

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| **Scenario** | 01 — Bibi's First Eid Booking with Tito |
| **Page Number** | 1.4 |
| **Platform** | Mobile (PWA, mid-range Android) |

## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Convert the shortlist tap into a confident booking intent by giving Bibi everything she needs to trust Rashid with Tito — demonstrated-Fit evidence, verified reviews with photos, capacity for her dates, and the booking CTA — all visible without scrolling past the fold.

**Entry Context:** Bibi tapped the top-ranked Tier-A provider (Rashid) from the curated shortlist on 1.3. URL: `petcare.bd/providers/rashid-gulshan-tier-a`. She is in compare mode against the other 3 shortlist options still visible in the back-stack.

**Exit Action:** Tap **"Book this provider"** → next page (1.5 — Pet Profile Management).

**On-Page Interactions:**
- Tap "Book this provider" — primary CTA · sticky on scroll
- Tap photo gallery — opens full-screen lightbox
- Tap any review — opens full review + photos
- Tap "See all 47 reviews with photos" — opens reviews list
- Tap "Show price breakdown" — modal opens (BDT 2,200/night × 9 nights = BDT 19,800, platform fee included, total)
- Tap "Message provider" — opens booking-scoped chat (only after a booking exists; here it shows "Available after you submit a booking request")
- Tap "Save for later" — bookmarks the provider to her dashboard

## Inherited Mental State
- **Hope:** This profile shows Rashid has demonstrably handled diabetic small dogs before — she can submit confidently
- **Worry:** The profile is generic "we love pets!" without any specific-Fit evidence; or worse, it claims capability but has no demonstrated history

## Page Composition

### Header (above the fold)
- **Hero photo:** Rashid's home or facility — a real photo, not stock
- **Provider name + Tier-A badge (gold)** with admin-introduced marker ("Personally vetted by PetCare")
- **Neighborhood:** Gulshan · 2.4 km from Banani · map snippet (tap to expand)
- **Status:** "Available for Eid-1 → Eid+9" (green dot · live capacity)
- **Quick stats row:** "6 yrs hosting · 4.9 ★ · 47 reviews with photos · 12 repeat owners"

### Demonstrated-Fit band (the differentiator — what PetCare shows that Facebook can't)
- **Title:** "Demonstrated experience" (Bangla: "প্রমাণিত অভিজ্ঞতা")
- **Subtitle:** "What Rashid has actually handled well on PetCare"
- **Cards (3–4 in a horizontal scroll):**
  - "12 diabetic small dogs handled successfully"
  - "6 cat boardings · 5 with medication needs"
  - "4 repeat owners · 3 with senior or special-care pets"
  - "Eid-week capacity honored in 4 of last 4 peak periods"
- Each card links to a filtered reviews list proving the claim

### Verified reviews with photos (the headline trust signal)
- **Filter chips:** All · With photos · Diabetic care · Senior pets · Repeat owners
- **First 3 reviews (expanded):** Star rating + reviewer first name + neighborhood + dates + photo grid + written review
- **Reviewer-meta line:** "Bibi can see photos from the actual stay"
- **CTA:** "See all 47 reviews with photos"

### Care setup details
- **Facility:** "Dedicated pet room · Catio (outdoor enclosed cat space) · Quiet separate room for special-care pets"
- **Species accepted:** Dogs (up to 12 kg) · Cats · Senior pets · Diabetic pets
- **Self-declared capability:** "Basic insulin administration · Oral medication · Eye drops · Special diet"
- **Vet affiliation:** "Vet on call via Dr. [Name], Gulshan Veterinary (1.2 km)"

### Pricing + booking CTA (sticky bottom)
- **Price:** "BDT 2,200 / night · 9 nights = BDT 19,800"
- **CTA:** **"Book this provider"** (primary, sticky)
- **Secondary:** "Save for later"
- **Helper text:** "Provider typically confirms within 2 hours · No charge until provider confirms"

### Below-fold (optional, if user scrolls)
- Cancellation policy summary (link to full)
- Vet-clinic directory (lightweight, per Brief §3) — names only, for stay-time lookup
- Provider's response-time stat ("Replies to requests in 1h 45m median")
- Profile completeness indicator (100%)

## State Variations

| State | Trigger | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| **Default** | Page load | Full profile as above · "Book this provider" sticky CTA |
| **Capacity-just-changed** | Provider toggled availability | "Just changed availability — still available for your dates" banner (or red dot if now unavailable) |
| **No reviews yet** | New Tier-A provider | "No verified reviews yet — see Rashid's demonstrated-Fit evidence above" |
| **Profile incomplete** | Provider hasn't finished editing | "Profile completeness: 70% · [See what's missing]" · booking still possible |
| **User has saved this provider** | Returning visit | "Saved" indicator + "Book this provider" |
| **User has booked this provider before** | Returning customer | "You've booked Rashid before · [Book again]" replaces standard CTA — Scenario 02 climax feed |

---

## What This Page Must NOT Do

- **No average-star-rating-only signal** — average star rating is NOT optimized (SM-C3 counter-metric); reviews with photos + demonstrated-Fit are the real signals
- **No self-declared capability masquerading as demonstrated-Fit** — the brief is explicit: demonstrated-care-fit over self-declared capability (Brief §2 Why-Win)
- **No stock photos** — provider photos must be real (Tier-A badge is meaningless without visible facility)
- **No "Sponsored" or "Boosted" labels** — paid placement is structurally excluded (Brief §3)
- **No booking without capacity confirmation** — the "Book this provider" CTA must validate against date-level capacity before submission (FR-12)
