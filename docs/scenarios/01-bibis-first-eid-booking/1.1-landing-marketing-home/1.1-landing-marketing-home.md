# 1.1 — Landing / Marketing Home

## Page Metadata

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| **Scenario** | 01 — Bibi's First Eid Booking with Tito |
| **Page Number** | 1.1 |
| **Platform** | Mobile (PWA, mid-range Android) |

## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Convert a curious mobile searcher (Bibi, just Googled "pet boarding Banani Dhaka diabetic dog") into a signed-in user with a confirmed booking target — without wasting her evening.

**Entry Context:** Bibi is on her phone in the Banani apartment, evening. She tapped the top organic Google result after a friend mentioned PetCare at dinner. URL: `petcare.bd/landing`.

**Exit Action:** Tap **"Find care near me"** → next page (1.2 — Owner sign-in).

**On-Page Interactions:**
- Scroll through value-prop sections (no taps required)
- Tap "How PetCare works" — opens a 3-step explainer modal (curated supply · verified reviews · care history)
- Tap language toggle (Bangla / English) — top right, persists choice via cookie
- Tap "I'm a provider" — switches to provider entry flow (out of this scenario)

---

## User Situation (from Q3)

**Persona:** Bibi the Banani-Booker, late-30s, Banani apartment, dual-income BDT 80k+, 4-year owner of Tito (diabetic Shih Tzu, 8 months post-diagnosis). WhatsApp-vendor loop has failed her twice already.

**Real-life moment:** Evening, 3 weeks before Eid. Sofa, one hand on Tito, husband packing in the background. She has 20 minutes before she has to step away to deal with the kids.

## Mental State (from Q4)

- **Hope:** PetCare shows her what providers have *demonstrably* handled well for diabetic small dogs — not just generic "we accept special care" claims.
- **Worry:** This is another generic classifieds page that wastes her evening and ends in another WhatsApp dead-end. If the landing page doesn't signal special-care credibility in the first scroll, she's gone.

## Discovery Method (from Q6)

Google search "pet boarding Banani Dhaka diabetic dog" → top organic result is petcare.bd. Bibi is comparing this against Facebook groups and Bikroy classifieds — PetCare has roughly 8 seconds to win the credibility battle.

---

## Page Composition (anchored to PRD FRs)

### Hero section (above the fold)
- **Headline (Bangla default + English toggle):** "Special-care pets, structured care, verified reviews."
- **Sub-headline:** "Dhaka's curated marketplace for dogs and cats with specific or special-care needs. Vetted providers, structured intake, daily updates from the stay."
- **Primary CTA:** **"Find care near me"** (FR-13 — defaults to her location if browser grants permission; otherwise launches location picker)
- **Secondary CTA:** "How PetCare works" (modal — not the path)
- **Trust strip below hero:** Three small badges — "Tier-A admin-vetted providers" · "Verified reviews with photos from the stay" · "Daily updates while your pet is there"

### Mid-page sections (scroll)
1. **For special-care pets:** Examples of what "structured intake" captures — insulin, allergies, anxiety, special diet, senior care. (Signals: FR-2, FR-6)
2. **From real Dhaka stays:** 3-card grid of anonymized-but-real-feeling review snippets with photos. (Signals: FR-19, FR-21, FR-43 — Tier-A demonstrated-Fit)
3. **How it works:** 3-step visual (Search → Confirm → Daily Updates + Verified Review). Single tap-to-expand details.
4. **Provider perspective:** "Are you a home-sitter or boarding facility? Apply to join PetCare." (Out-of-scenario CTA)

### Footer
- Trust line: "PetCare is not a veterinary boarding service. We support normal pet care and basic medication administration where the provider explicitly agrees."
- Links: Bangla-first content toggle · About · Pre-launch supply assessment · Customer support
- Legal: Animal Welfare Act 2017 compliance · Data Protection (draft 2023) · Trade license

---

## State Variations

- **Default (this scenario):** Bangla, location permission prompted
- **Returning user:** Same page, but "Find care near me" CTA shifts to "Continue to your shortlist" (logged in via cookie)
- **Expat locale detected (EN, USD pricing seen in browser):** Headline shows English + adds "Bilingual providers in Gulshan–Baridhara" sub-line
- **Pre-launch state (waiting on supply assessment):** Hero shows "We're launching in Gulshan, Banani, Baridhara — join the waitlist" with structured-intake form

---

## What This Page Must NOT Do

- **No Facebook-pixel retargeting hooks** — the brief is explicit: PetCare is not "convert users at any cost," it is the curated-trust surface. (Brand-voice adaptation: empowering, organic, not "convert users" — per TM Handover open item #5.)
- **No paid placement for providers** — even on the landing page, the hero trust strip names no provider by name. Tier-A visibility happens post-sign-in.
- **No medical / clinical care claims** — the footer trust line keeps this explicit (Brief §6 scope guardrail).
