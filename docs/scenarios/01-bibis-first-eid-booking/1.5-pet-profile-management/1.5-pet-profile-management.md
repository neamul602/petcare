# 1.5 — Pet Profile Management — Tito's First Profile

## Page Metadata

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| **Scenario** | 01 — Bibi's First Eid Booking with Tito |
| **Page Number** | 1.5 |
| **Platform** | Mobile (PWA, mid-range Android) |

## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Capture Tito's complete care profile in a single form so it can be attached to the booking, persisted into PetCare's care-history record, and re-used for the second booking (Scenario 02 climax).

**Entry Context:** Bibi tapped "Book this provider" on 1.4. This is her first booking on PetCare — Tito has no existing profile. The page is a long-form but single-screen form on mobile (scroll within sections).

**Exit Action:** Tap **"Save Tito's profile & continue"** → next page (1.6 — Booking Configuration).

**On-Page Interactions:**
- Tap section headers — collapse/expand
- Tap photo upload — opens camera roll (1 photo minimum, 5 max)
- Tap "Add another condition" — appends a new condition row
- Toggle medication switches — opens dosage + timing sub-form
- Tap "Save & continue" — primary CTA, sticky on scroll

---

## Inherited Mental State
- **Hope:** The form is structured (not free-text only) — Tito's diabetes, kibble type, and anxiety triggers all have explicit fields
- **Worry:** Another free-text "tell us about your pet" box where she has to compose a paragraph; or the form requires info she doesn't have on hand (vet records upload)

## Page Composition

### Header
- **Pet name:** "Tito" (default · editable)
- **Photo upload zone:** "Add Tito's photo" — 1 minimum, 5 max, 5 MB each · required for this profile (provider recognition)
- **Pet-type selector:** Dog ✓ (locked from shortlist filter) · Cat selector visible but greyed

### Section 1 — Basic info
- Species (locked: Dog)
- Breed (autocomplete): Shih Tzu ✓
- Age: 4 years
- Weight: 7 kg
- Sex: Male · Neutered ✓
- Vaccinations: Up to date ✓ · (toggle: not up to date / unknown / N/A)
- Microchip ID: optional

### Section 2 — Behavioural notes
- Energy level: Calm / Moderate / High (radio)
- Good with: Other dogs (size filter) · Cats · Children · Strangers (multi-toggle)
- Behaviour triggers: "Wary of larger dogs" · "Stressed by loud fireworks" · (free-text add)

### Section 3 — Medical conditions (the critical special-care section)
- **Active conditions:** Diabetes (8 months) ✓ · (add another)
- **For each condition:**
  - Diagnosis date (approximate ok)
  - Severity (Mild / Moderate / Severe)
  - Vet contact (name + clinic + phone — required if condition = Moderate or Severe)
  - Daily care notes (free-text, 500 char)

### Section 4 — Medication
- **Active medications:** Insulin (8am + 8pm) ✓
- **For each medication:**
  - Name + dose (e.g. "Insulin — 2 units")
  - Frequency (e.g. "Twice daily at 8am and 8pm")
  - Administration route (oral / injection / topical / drops)
  - Notes (e.g. "Keep insulin refrigerated; provider should have cooler pack")

### Section 5 — Diet
- **Food type:** Low-carb kibble ✓ · (Wet food / Raw / Home-cooked / Mixed)
- **Brand:** Specific brand selector (autocomplete from common BD brands)
- **Feeding schedule:** 2 meals/day · 8am + 8pm
- **Treats:** "Only vet-approved diabetic treats; no table food"
- **Allergies:** None known ✓ · (multi-toggle: Chicken / Beef / Grain / Dairy / Other)

### Section 6 — Special-attention needs
- **Anxiety triggers:** "Loud fireworks (Eid period is high-stress)" ✓
- **Comfort items:** "His small blanket from home — please don't wash with scented detergent"
- **Exercise needs:** "Short leash walk once daily is fine; not high-energy"

### Footer
- **Helper text:** "Pet profile is saved to Tito's record. Future bookings will pre-fill from this profile."
- **Primary CTA:** **"Save Tito's profile & continue"** (sticky on scroll)
- **Secondary:** "Save as draft" (returns to dashboard)

## State Variations

| State | Trigger | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| **Default empty form** | Page load | All fields empty · "Save" CTA disabled until required fields filled |
| **Required-field warning** | User taps Save with missing required | Inline errors on missing fields · scrolls to first error |
| **Autocomplete-breed** | User types breed | Suggestions appear · tap to fill |
| **Condition-severity-Medium** | User selects Moderate or Severe | Vet contact becomes required · inline validation |
| **Photo-required** | User has not added photo | "Add at least 1 photo" inline hint · Save CTA disabled |
| **Returning-user + existing pet** | Second booking flow (Scenario 02) | Form pre-fills from Tito's saved profile · "Edit" toggles · "Book again" path opens (Scenario 02 climax) |
| **Multi-pet household** | User has multiple pet profiles | Pet selector at top · "Add another pet" CTA |

---

## What This Page Must NOT Do

- **No free-text-only "tell us about your pet" box** — every important field is structured (FR-2, FR-6)
- **No vet-records upload requirement** — the brief is explicit: special-care support yes, clinical care no (Brief §1 scope guardrail); structured condition fields suffice, no medical records upload
- **No document upload for owner identity** — that's at sign-in (1.2) and only phone OTP + name; vet contact is a phone number, not a license
- **No "complete your profile to 100%" pressure** — completion is the booking's concern, not gamification
