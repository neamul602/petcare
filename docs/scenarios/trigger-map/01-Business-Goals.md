# Business Goals & Objectives

> Strategic goals and measurable objectives for PetCare

**Document:** Trigger Map — Business Goals
**Created:** 2026-08-30
**Status:** COMPLETE

## Vision

PetCare aims to be the trusted, bookable marketplace in Dhaka that an owner with a special-care pet reaches for first — not the loudest, cheapest, or largest, but the one whose reputation is built on what providers have demonstrably handled well across species and care needs over time.

---

## Business Objectives

### ⭐ PRIMARY GOAL: Curated-Trust Moat Compounds Through Repeat Bookings On-Platform (THE ENGINE)

- **Statement:** Build the curated Dhaka marketplace whose moat is not the supply list or the brand but the accumulated care history that only grows when owners and providers keep transacting on the platform rather than defecting to WhatsApp after the first booking.
- **Metric:** Repeat-booking rate on-platform (SM-2) and provider-side retention (SM-3). Validated secondarily by Second-Booking Time (SM-15) and verified-review rate (SM-10).
- **Target:** Repeat-booking share becomes the dominant booking pattern within the pilot cohort. *Numerical targets deliberately deferred until after the first pilot cycle per Brief §4 / PRD §11 A-21.*
- **Timeline:** Compounding effect measured across the pilot's first six months; meaningful share inflection expected by month 4–6 of steady-state operation.
- **Impact:** This drives ALL other objectives. Without the curated-trust moat compounding in-platform, PetCare becomes a search-engine-shaped directory and the marketplace fails on the dimension that justifies its existence.
- **Anchored to:** FR-1, FR-7, FR-8, FR-11, FR-15, FR-26, FR-43, FR-44, FR-45, FR-48, FR-50 · SM-2, SM-3, SM-15.

---

### 🚀 SECONDARY GOALS (Driven by the Primary Moat)

The secondary objectives exist because the primary moat has to clear operational reality — volume has to fund the Admin, the supply has to stay curated, and the 1 / 25 / 75 ceiling has to hold without forcing Ayaat into a corner where service quality collapses.

#### Objective 1: Booking Volume Clears Operational Costs Without Breaching the 1 / 25 / 75 Ceiling

- **Statement:** Aggregate monthly bookings clear the fixed cost of a single Admin's time and the per-booking support burden while Ayaat remains inside the operating ceiling.
- **Metric:** Bookings per month per active Provider (SM-13), per-neighborhood density (SM-14), Admin Load vs. Operating Ceiling (SM-18), Admin Intervention Rate (SM-16), % of Bookings Completed Without Any Admin Touch (SM-17).
- **Target:** Operational cost cleared at the steady-state cohort size; Ayaat stays below 25 active Providers and 75 Completed Bookings per month. *Numerical thresholds deferred per A-21.*
- **Timeline:** Tracked monthly from launch; ceiling breach treated as a launch-blocking signal per PRD §11.
- **Anchored to:** FR-4, FR-30, FR-32, FR-35, FR-38 · SM-1, SM-13, SM-14, SM-18, SM-19, SM-20.

#### Objective 2: Acute-Event Response Becomes the Launch-Blocking Trust Signal

- **Statement:** When an acute event happens during a stay, PetCare's runbook converts what would be a trust-breaker in the WhatsApp market into a trust-builder, because the structured escalation path closes faster than panicked DMs ever could.
- **Metric:** Acute-Event Time-to-Admin-Page (SM-20) and Acute-Event Post-Mortem Closure Rate (SM-19).
- **Target:** Median time-to-admin-page under the pilot-defined threshold; >95% post-mortem closure rate. *Numerical thresholds deferred per A-21.*
- **Timeline:** Runbook reviewed after every acute event during the pilot; mechanism validated by end of pilot phase 1.
- **Anchored to:** FR-26, FR-27, FR-28, FR-29, FR-30, FR-32, FR-34, FR-45 · SM-19, SM-20.

#### Objective 3: Off-Platform Defection Stays Visible and Manageable

- **Statement:** When owners and providers are tempted to take the second booking to WhatsApp to skip the platform fee, PetCare's soft-signal capture makes the defection observable to Ayaat and the on-platform repeat-value tooling makes staying on-platform more valuable than the discount.
- **Metric:** Platform-booking share (SM-C1 counter-metric — do not optimize, but monitor), provider platform-share (SM-C2 — same), care-record completeness (SM-C5 — same).
- **Target:** Off-platform defection attempts visible in the admin dashboard within the soft-signal capture window; second-booking discount attempts declined because the on-platform repeat-value tooling wins on net convenience. *Numerical thresholds deferred per A-21.*
- **Timeline:** Tracked across the pilot; mechanism validated when second-booking attempts decline without admin intervention.
- **Anchored to:** FR-10, FR-36, FR-48, FR-49, FR-50 · A-14.

### 🌱 TERTIARY GOALS (Real-World Benefits for Members)

These are the opportunities PetCare creates FOR its community members — owners, providers, and admin operators — once the Primary moat is compounding and the Secondary operational discipline is holding. They are downstream of the engine, not separate from it.

#### Objective 4: Demonstrated Care Fit Becomes the Default Trust Signal

- **Statement:** The trust signal shifts from "provider says they can handle X" (Capability) to "platform shows what providers have actually handled well across species and care needs" (Demonstrated Care Fit). Owners reach for PetCare first on their first search because the first search already ranks by demonstrated fit, not by self-declared claims.
- **Metric:** Decline-Reason Distribution (SM-7) and Review-With-Photos Rate (SM-11) — the two signals that prove Demonstrated Care Fit is replacing Capability at the point of decision.
- **Target:** Demonstrated care-history is the default presentation on every Tier-A provider profile; self-declared capability is presented only as a fallback when no history exists yet. *Numerical thresholds deferred per A-21.*
- **Timeline:** Capability-to-Fit transition tracked across the pilot; full replacement targeted by the end of pilot phase 1.
- **Benefit to Members:** Owners stop re-litigating Tito's diabetes at every booking; providers stop being sorted by claims they can't back up; the platform stops optimizing for the wrong signal (SM-C3 — Average Star Rating as a reputation signal is explicitly NOT optimized).
- **Anchored to:** FR-11, FR-21, FR-43, FR-44, FR-49 · SM-7, SM-11.

#### Objective 5: Owners Re-Experience Pet Care as a Shared, Low-Anxiety Practice

- **Statement:** The owner-side emotional transformation — from "another WhatsApp-vendor gamble with Tito's insulin curve" to "first-search confidence because PetCare already shows what providers have demonstrably handled well." The structured Daily Updates, the verified reviews with photos, and the second-booking speed are all designed to lower the anxiety that the WhatsApp loop currently amplifies.
- **Metric:** Search-to-Request Conversion Rate (SM-4), Daily-Update Posting Rate during Active Stays (SM-9), Verified-Review Rate (SM-10).
- **Target:** Search-to-request conversion strengthens across the pilot; daily updates become expected rather than exceptional. *Numerical thresholds deferred per A-21.*
- **Timeline:** Owner-experience transformation tracked across the pilot; full shift expected once repeat-booking share inflects (Primary Objective 1's success signal).
- **Benefit to Members:** Owners stop burning emotional capital on every booking; the act of leaving a pet for a stay stops feeling like a hand-off into the unknown.
- **Anchored to:** FR-1, FR-9, FR-15, FR-19, FR-21, FR-43, FR-45 · SM-4, SM-9, SM-10.

#### Objective 6: Providers Build a Portable Care-Record Reputation That Compounds

- **Statement:** Every special-case Rashid handles well on PetCare becomes part of his demonstrated profile, so the right owners find him without Rashid having to advertise. His care-record reputation compounds across species and care needs; Rashid's slow off-peak weeks stop threatening his livelihood.
- **Metric:** Provider-Side Retention (SM-3), Provider Utilization by Tier (SM-13), Provider Confirmation Rate (SM-6).
- **Target:** Providers who complete a first booking accept and complete a second on-platform at a rate that proves the curated-supply moat is functioning for them, not just for owners. *Numerical thresholds deferred per A-21.*
- **Timeline:** Provider-experience transformation tracked across the pilot; portable reputation becomes the differentiator by end of pilot phase 1.
- **Benefit to Members:** Rashid stops being a commodity-vendor and becomes a known-quantity specialist; off-peak troughs become survivable because the platform channels the right Bookings to him.
- **Anchored to:** FR-16, FR-19, FR-20, FR-21, FR-36 · SM-3, SM-6, SM-13.

## Counter-Metrics the Platform Will NOT Optimize For

These are as load-bearing as the primary metrics. They prevent the architect from optimizing the wrong thing and the developer from gaming the wrong target.

- **SM-C1: GMV or Booking-revenue as a v1 success metric.** v1 exists to validate the curated-trust moat; premature revenue targets distort Provider approval and Review moderation. *Counterbalances SM-1, SM-13.*
- **SM-C2: Active-User counts as a v1 success metric.** The Dhaka supply pool is small; chasing downloads produces a directory, not a curated marketplace. *Counterbalances SM-4, SM-5.*
- **SM-C3: Average Star Rating as the Provider reputation signal.** A single star rating averages across species and special-care needs and obscures Demonstrated Care Fit. *Counterbalances SM-11 and the demonstrated-care-fit principle.*
- **SM-C4: Booking Volume per Launch Neighborhood as the Launch-readiness signal.** Volume alone does not validate density; the right density signal is Completed-and-Reviewed Bookings per neighborhood. *Counterbalances SM-14.*
- **SM-C5: Provider Earnings as a Provider-success signal in v1.** Provider earnings are a v2+ health metric; in v1, the curated-supply model accepts the off-peak trough as a feature. *Counterbalances SM-13.*

## Explicitly NOT Measured in v1

Per PRD §11: targets and thresholds for SM-1 through SM-20 are architecture-stage / operational decisions; the PRD names what to measure and why. The brief explicitly states that "anything that would force us to invent a target number today — booking volume, MAU, conversion rate, NPS, revenue — is deferred to the PRD, where it can be tied to a realistic baseline after the first pilot cycle." This document preserves that discipline.
