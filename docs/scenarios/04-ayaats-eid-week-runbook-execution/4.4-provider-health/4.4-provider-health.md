# 4.4 — Provider Health Surface

## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Ayaat sees all 18 active providers' quality signals at a glance — response rate, daily-update rate, care-record completeness, dispute history — so she catches problems before they become incidents.

**Exit Action:** Tap any provider → provider detail · Tap "Flag for follow-up" → adds to Ayaat's task list.

## Page Composition

### Provider grid (18 active)
Each card shows:
- Name + Tier badge · response rate · daily-update rate · care-record completeness · dispute count · last-booking date

### Quality flags
- 🟢 Healthy · 🟡 Watch · 🔴 Flag (soft signals before they become incidents)

### Filter / sort
- By quality flag · by response time · by tier · by neighborhood

## State Variations

- **Watch-list-non-empty**: "3 providers on watch" banner
- **Flag-list-non-empty**: "1 provider flagged" banner · Ayaat sees next action
