# 4.4 · Spec — Provider Health Surface

> All 18 active providers' quality signals at a glance — response rate, daily-update rate, care-record completeness, dispute count, last-booking date. Catch problems before they become incidents.

## URL & Routing

- **Route:** `/admin/providers` (table view, this surface) + `/admin/providers/:id` (drill-down, out of scope)
- **Auth gate:** `admin` role
- **Polling:** every 5min for health-signal updates

## Design Sections

**D1. Header** — admin nav + health summary line (counts by quality flag)

**D2. Conditional banners** — Watch-list non-empty + Flag-list non-empty banners · Ayaat sees next action

**D3. Filter + sort row** — `{filter-chip}` · horizontal scroll · 6 filter options · sort selector

**D4. Provider grid** — `{card.muted}` per provider · quality flag dot · key metrics · last-booking recency · CTAs

## On-Page State Machine

- **State 1 (default — all healthy):** No banners · all green dots · neutral filter state
- **State 2 (watch-list-non-empty):** Warning banner · at least 1 yellow dot · "Open watch list" CTA
- **State 3 (flag-list-non-empty):** Destructive banner · at least 1 red dot · "Investigate" CTA primary
- **State 4 (filtered view):** Filter chip selected · grid re-renders to matching providers · count updates
- **State 5 (flag-for-followup added):** Card highlighted · toast · task list updates

## Accessibility

- Header: `<header>` semantic · summary `<dl>` semantic
- Watch/Flag banners: `role="status" aria-live="polite"` announces changes
- Filter chips: `role="button" aria-pressed` · keyboard-navigable
- Sort selector: native `<select>` · keyboard-friendly
- Provider cards: `<article>` semantic · CTA primary focusable
- Quality flag dot: `aria-label="Healthy"` / "Watch" / "Flagged" · not just color
- Metric rows: `<dl>` semantic per card · consistent structure
- All CTAs ≥48px tap target

## What This Page Must NOT Do

- No auto-flagging based on single signal (multi-signal threshold required)
- No provider comparison ("Rashid ranks #5 of 18 by response rate")
- No public visibility of these health signals (admin-only)
- No penalty for being Tier-B in v1 (tier is quality, not penalty)
- No pressure CTAs ("Investigate this provider before they damage the platform")
