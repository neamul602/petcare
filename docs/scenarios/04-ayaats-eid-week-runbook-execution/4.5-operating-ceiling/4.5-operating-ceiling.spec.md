# 4.5 · Spec — Operating Ceiling Dashboard

> Real-time view of Admin Load vs. the 1/25/75 operating ceiling. Throttles new supply before service quality collapses. **The dashboard that prevents Ayaat Fear #2 (operating-ceiling breach).**

## URL & Routing

- **Route:** `/admin/ceiling` · desktop-primary · deep-linkable from queue banner
- **Auth gate:** `admin` role
- **Polling:** every 5min for ceiling-axis updates · push subscription on `admin.ceiling` channel

## Design Sections

## On-Page State Machine

- **State 1 (below-ceiling — current):** All axes green · no throttle active · trending shows safe trajectory
- **State 2 (near-ceiling):** At least 1 axis above 80% · that axis card turns orange · throttle suggestion appears
- **State 3 (breached):** At least 1 axis at/above 100% · axis card turns red · Apply Throttle CTA primary · escalation banner: "Multi-admin RBAC recommended"
- **State 4 (acute-events-active):** Acute axis shows real-time binding · "1 active" · CTA routes to 4.2 runbook
- **State 5 (throttle-active):** Applied toggles highlighted · "Throttle active since {time}" indicator · Reset baseline CTA visible
- **State 6 (throttle-confirm):** Apply modal · confirms throttles · logs decision

## Accessibility

- Header: `<header>` semantic · 3-axis summary `<dl>` semantic
- Axis cards: `<section>` semantic · each `<dl>` with `<dt>`/`<dd>` · `aria-label="Active providers: 18 of 25, 72 percent, healthy"`
- Trending chart: `<figure>` with `<figcaption>` · textual narrative alongside visual
- Throttle toggles: `role="switch" aria-checked` · keyboard-operable
- Escalation banner: `role="alert"` announces ceiling breach
- Apply modal: `role="alertdialog"` · confirms throttle changes
- All CTAs ≥48px tap target

## What This Page Must NOT Do

- No auto-throttling (Ayaat decides when to apply)
- No hiding approaching-ceiling warnings (transparent signals)
- No "ignore for 7 days" snooze — throttle decisions are intentional
- No comparing to other operators (1 admin is the v1 ceiling; no comparison)
- No pressure CTAs ("Throttle now to avoid disaster")
