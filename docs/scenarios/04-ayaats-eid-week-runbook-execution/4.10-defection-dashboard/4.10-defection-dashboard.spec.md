# 4.10 · Spec — Off-Platform Defection Detection Dashboard

> Soft signals that owners/providers are moving the next booking to WhatsApp — visible before the moat starts dissolving. **The shared cross-group driver's signal surface** (TM cross-group driver) + **Ayaat Fear #3 (off-platform signal invisible):** the surface that makes it visible.

## URL & Routing

- **Route:** `/admin/defection` · desktop-primary · deep-linkable from queue dashboard's trend-watch section
- **Auth gate:** `admin` role
- **Polling:** every 6hr for trend-watch updates · push subscription on `defection.signals` channel

**D4. Soft signals (3 cards)** — `{card.muted}` · one card per category · yellow/red dots per signal · playbook CTA per signal

## On-Page State Machine

- **State 1 (clean-state — current):** No trending banner · signals shown · no patterns detected
- **State 2 (attrition-period):** Trending banner visible · "trending down {N}%" · playbook CTA primary
- **State 3 (pattern-detected):** At least 1 cross-side signal highlighted with 🔴 · intervention CTA primary
- **State 4 (filter-applied):** Only matching signal categories visible · count updates
- **State 5 (intervention-launched):** Playbook opened (out-of-scope) · signal marked "intervention in progress" · audit-log entry created
- **State 6 (intervention-completed):** Signal archived · cross-link to 4.7 audit-log · next-period data refreshes

## Accessibility

- Header: `<header>` semantic · summary `<dl>` semantic
- Trending banner: `role="status" aria-live="polite"` · announces trend change
- Filter chips: `role="button" aria-pressed"` · keyboard-navigable
- Signal category cards: `<section>` semantic · each `<ul>` of signals
- Signal rows: `<li>` semantic · playbook CTA focusable
- Signal dots: `aria-label="Soft signal" / "Cross-side signal"` · not just color
- Playbook CTA: `aria-label="Open intervention playbook for {signal}"`
- All CTAs ≥48px tap target

## What This Page Must NOT Do

- No revealing which owner/provider specifically (aggregate signals only — privacy)
- No "spy on chat" framing — soft signals, not surveillance
- No pressure CTAs on owners/providers ("You're losing customers")
- No comparison to other platforms ("WhatsApp bookings are up elsewhere")
- No auto-intervention (every nudge is admin-initiated)
- No revealing defection to the provider being nudged
- No sharing signals with other owners/providers
