# 4.7 · Spec — Review Moderation Queue

> Moderation for cause only — never for star-rating optimization. **SM-C3 explicit counter-metric:** average star rating is NOT a goal. Removing a critical review requires the same reason rigor as declining an application.

## URL & Routing

- **Route:** `/admin/reviews/moderation` · desktop-primary · deep-linkable from queue dashboard's review-queue section
- **Auth gate:** `admin` role
- **Polling:** every 5min for new pending reviews · push subscription on `review.moderation` channel

## On-Page State Machine

- **State 1 (default — pending queue):** All pending reviews shown · 5 filter options · sort applied
- **State 2 (flagged-only):** Filter applied · only flagged reviews visible · count badge updates
- **State 3 (consistency-detected):** Ayaat's prior-decision pattern surfaced inline · "consistent with prior decisions" or "inconsistent — explain deviation"
- **State 4 (SM-C3-warning-shown):** Critical reviews (3 stars and below) display the warning banner · still editable, but Ayaat must explicitly acknowledge
- **State 5 (edit-modal-open):** Edit modal with textarea · redact selection · preview · Save · logs what was redacted
- **State 6 (remove-modal-open):** Reason taxonomy modal · required · Save logs decision · cross-link to audit log
- **State 7 (bulk-pattern-detected):** Banner · "5 reviews of similar pattern detected this week" · bulk-view CTA

## Accessibility

- Header: `<header>` semantic · summary `<dl>` semantic
- Policy banner: `role="note"` · SM-C3 link is keyboard-accessible
- Filter chips: `role="button" aria-pressed` · keyboard-navigable
- Review cards: `<article>` semantic · actions are `<button>` elements
- Rating stars: `aria-label="{N} of 5 stars"` · not just visual
- Automated flags: `aria-label="{flag-type}"` · not just color
- Consistency check: `aria-describedby` on Approve CTA
- SM-C3 warning: `role="note"` · explicit acknowledgment checkbox for critical reviews
- Edit modal: `role="dialog"` · textarea `aria-required="false"`
- Remove modal: `role="alertdialog"` · reason `aria-required="true"`
- All CTAs ≥48px tap target

## What This Page Must NOT Do

- No removing critical reviews without policy-violation reason (SM-C3 protection)
- No star-rating-only display metrics (avg rating not shown as a goal)
- No bulk-remove of critical reviews without per-review acknowledgment
- No "this is hurting our average rating" framing anywhere
- No auto-removal of low-effort reviews (low-effort is a flag, not an auto-action)
- No comparison to other providers' review profiles
