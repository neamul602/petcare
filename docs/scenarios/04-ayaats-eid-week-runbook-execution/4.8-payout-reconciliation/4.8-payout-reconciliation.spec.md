# 4.8 · Spec — Payout Reconciliation View

> Monthly finance close — reconcile MFS settlements against provider payouts. Discrepancy detection before period-closing bulk action. **No payment-modification authority from this surface** — that lives in accounting tooling. This view produces the reconciled ledger Ayaat hands off.

## URL & Routing

- **Route:** `/admin/payouts/reconciliation` · desktop-primary · deep-linkable from queue dashboard's finance section
- **Auth gate:** `admin` role
- **Polling:** every 30min for MFS settlement updates · push subscription on `payout.reconciliation` channel

## On-Page State Machine

- **State 1 (period-open — current):** Bookings reconciled as they complete · period close CTA enabled only when all reconciled
- **State 2 (period-mismatch-detected):** Mismatch delta >0 · Mark reconciled CTA disabled · escalate CTA primary · discrepancy investigation visible
- **State 3 (period-frozen-payouts-present):** Disputes from 4.6 surface as frozen · accounted for in close · not blocking
- **State 4 (period-closing-confirm):** Confirm modal · "Mark Aug 2026 reconciled? Once marked, no further edits from this surface" · logs decision
- **State 5 (period-closed):** Period archived · CSV exported · cross-link to audit log · next-period table loads
- **State 6 (discrepancy-escalated):** Platform team notified · investigation notes preserved · cross-link to platform team queue
- **State 7 (all-matched-clean):** No mismatch · "✓ All matched · ready to close" · Mark reconciled CTA active

## What This Page Must NOT Do

- No payment-modification authority (this view reconciles, doesn't modify)
- No marking reconciled while mismatch present (gate enforced)
- No public visibility (admin-only, finance-sensitive)
- No comparison to other periods in a way that pressures ("last month closed faster")
- No "auto-reconcile" — every line is human-reviewed
- No penalty CTA on mismatch — escalate, don't blame
