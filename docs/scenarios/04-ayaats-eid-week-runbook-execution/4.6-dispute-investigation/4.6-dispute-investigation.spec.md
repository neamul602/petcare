# 4.6 · Spec — Dispute Investigation View

> Independent of reviews. Payout freeze logic enforced. Reviews + disputes stay as separate records per Brief §3.

- **Auth gate:** `admin` role
- **Dispute window:** 7 days from booking completion · countdown shown in header

## On-Page State Machine

- **State 1 (just-opened — current):** SLA countdown · initial-investigation CTA prominent · booking history expanded
- **State 2 (awaiting-provider-response):** Provider notified · "Awaiting response · {N}h elapsed" · Ayaat can add notes meanwhile
- **State 3 (resolution-ready):** All evidence reviewed · resolution block enabled · Resolve CTA active
- **State 4 (closed-with-payout-release):** Resolved · payout released · post-mortem entry auto-created (if applicable) · dispute archived
- **State 5 (closed-with-payout-hold):** Resolved · payout remains frozen · post-mortem entry created · dispute archived · freeze visible in 4.8 reconciliation
- **State 6 (dispute-window-expired):** Auto-archive · default to payout release · manual override possible

## Accessibility

- Header: `<header>` semantic · SLA `aria-label="{N} days remaining"`
- Booking history: `<dl>` semantic · `<dt>`/`<dd>` per row
- Independent-records notice: `role="note"` · `aria-label="Reminder: reviews and disputes are independent records"`
- Investigation timeline: `<ol>` semantic · entries chronological
- Payout decision: `<fieldset>` with `<legend>` · radio group · arrow-key navigable
- Resolution reason: `aria-required="true"` · `<textarea>`
- Resolve CTA: `aria-disabled="true"` until valid · `aria-describedby` explains why
- All CTAs ≥48px tap target

## What This Page Must NOT Do

- No removing reviews because of dispute (independent records per Brief §3)
- No resolving without reason (required)
- No auto-resolution (every resolution is Ayaat's decision)
- No comparison to other disputes ("similar cases resolved this way")
- No pressure CTAs ("Resolve quickly to release payout")
