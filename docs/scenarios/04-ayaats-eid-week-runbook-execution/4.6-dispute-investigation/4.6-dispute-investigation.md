**Page Purpose:** Ayaat investigates a "Report an issue" — independent of reviews; payout freeze logic enforced; review-and-dispute records stay independent.

**Exit Action:** Resolve (with payout release / hold decision) → dispute closes.

- Booking ref · owner · provider · reported issue category
