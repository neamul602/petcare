## The five validation sources (Step 38)

1. **Product brief and PRD**: the curated-trust, special-care-strong marketplace is demonstrated end to end (shortlist with reasons, Tier-A badge, structured care plan, insulin handling, vetting gate, no invented trust numbers). Deferred features are listed in `memory-bank/deferred-scope.md`.
2. **UI/UX**: `evidence/STEP-33-ui-validation/` (18 side-by-side comparisons and `validation.md` with every difference and its reason).
3. **Requirements**: every FR is named by a story or unit (`evidence/STEP-36-project-status.md`); 73 of 76 stories are implemented, 3 are deferred by decision.
4. **Acceptance criteria**: each bolt's `test-walkthrough.md` maps every acceptance criterion to its test; failure paths are in `evidence/STEP-35-failure-tests.md`.
5. **Tests**: 439 unit and API tests, 262 browser tests, zero WCAG 2.1 AA violations on every checked screen in both languages.

## Known limits (honest list)

- Booking chat, the notification system and inbox, notification preferences, real SMS, email and OTP delivery, account closure with re-verification, and the purge of expired booking-side records are not built (deferred, see `memory-bank/deferred-scope.md`).
- The running servers cannot move their clock, so the browser journey edits a booking's dates in the end-to-end database to make a stay "already happened"; all timing rules are proven with an injectable clock in the unit tests.
- The admin queue is the simpler layout (no KPI tiles, filters or paging) and its under-2-second load on a throttled profile is not separately measured.
- Offline holding of a daily update has no browser test; an automatic queue for a photo across a reload is not built.
- Workbook screenshots (`STEP-36-project-status.png`, `STEP-37-final-dashboard.png` and the like) are left for the project owner to capture.
